## Colors

Approximately 70% light, 25% navy, 5% blue. Electric blue belongs to artwork and accents. White on #008CFF fails AA at normal text size: buttons use functional blue #075BB5. These are shades of one accent.

## Typography

Sora for geometric, technical headings; Manrope for approachable body reading. Self-host both. Remove Fraunces from the active UI: the serif/dark-server direction was rejected. Sentence-case headings, no colored-word trick. Body measure under 75 characters.

## Layout

Mobile-first 320–1920px. Desktop max 1280px. White navigation with five in-page links, expansive light hero and edge-to-edge field photography. Asymmetric service layout, varied section proportions, mainland map. No page-hopping, identical tile grid, or all-accordion homepage. Utility surfaces only where grouping helps.

## Elevation & Depth

Photography, scale, and contrast. No decorative gradients, blur, universal shadows, or fake dashboards. Shadow only on the mobile modal.

## Shapes

4px buttons and 12px utility surfaces. Clean photograph edges. No pill-only UI. Preserve official artwork, do not redraw or distort it.

## Components

shadcn/Radix: Button, Sheet, Accordion, Input, Label, Textarea. Mobile Sheet closes on navigation, traps focus, supports Escape, restores focus. Tailark inspires composition, not copied templates. 21st.dev is research, not an installation authority. Magic MCP is unavailable. Aceternity, Magic UI and React Bits were evaluated: shaders, particles, rotating globes and flashy effects do not serve this brief.

- October service examples from Henry are owner-provided, not a new MCP validation. No rates, addresses, personal data, invented metrics, certifications or safety practices.
- Markets: TX, MN, NE/Omaha, VA, NC, LA/Lafayette, IL. No permanent offices or immediate capacity implied.
